About the Dev

Art Hoskins • Grind2Give

Who I am outside of crypto and why I started Grind2Give

Who I Am

I’m a coach and a life mentor. I come from a big family, and I also have a big family of my own. That’s a big reason why doing good for others means so much to me.

My life has been centered around faith, music, and community. I’ve spent years coaching young athletes, making music, and working with Greater Church Acworth. I’ve always had a heart for giving back, and that desire is what eventually led me here.

Why I Started Grind2Give

I got burned by pump-and-dump scams more than once. I saw how many coins are built to hurt people. So I created something different — a coin that helps children with life-threatening illnesses, and also gives a little back to the people who are part of it.

Other Projects

I run Grind2Give Creative Capital. Our mission is missionary, and our method is creativity.

I released an album called From the Streets to the Altar. It’s available on Apple Music, Spotify, Amazon Music, iHeart Radio, and other platforms.

I’ve also created faith-driven videos on YouTube, and I’m currently working on a children’s book.

Why I’m Doing This

Money isn’t everything to me. I make a comfortable living from my regular work, so I don’t need this project to survive. I get real joy from living my life — coaching, spending time with family, making music, and serving others.

My long-term goal is to keep helping children and their families who can’t afford the care they need.

Why You Can Trust Me

I’ve been through a lot in life, and it never changed my character. I’ve seen the good and the bad, and I’ve stayed consistent. I don’t think the good I try to do comes from me. I believe it comes from the guidance and teachings of the Holy Spirit that dwells in me. I’m not perfect, but I’m transparent, and I’m doing this for the right reasons.
