The Problem

What went wrong with the current coin

When the current $G2G contract was set up, the trading pair — the pool the coin trades through — was placed on the contract’s fee-exempt list. Every single trade passes through that pair. So even though the contract was built with a 10% buy tax (8% to charity, 1.5% to holders, 0.5% burned), the tax could never be collected. We verified it on-chain, transaction by transaction:

8,265.43 G2G
The charity pot has sat frozen at this amount since the beginning — it never grew.
Charity never paid out from trading
The charity wallet never received a single payment from trading.
Dividends never distributed to holders
Even though the contract was built to pay them, not one dividend ever went out.

Here is what that does not mean

Nobody lost money to this. Every buyer received 100% of the coins they bought — the tax simply never took its cut. No funds were taken, moved, or misused.

What was lost is the giving that should have happened. Every buy was supposed to send help to kids fighting cancer. It didn’t. That is what this launch fixes.

The Fix

Born Again: a new contract, built so the engine actually runs

A new $G2G contract — same name, same ticker, same mission, same charity wallet — on Base, trading on Uniswap V2. The pair will not sit on any fee-exempt list — there is no whitelist — so the tax collects the way it was always supposed to. Here is exactly where every trade goes:

7% tax on buys
  • Charity4%
    Paid in ETH to the public charity wallet, funding care and research for kids fighting cancer.
  • Holder dividends2%
    Paid in ETH to holders of 254,000 G2G or more. Auto-claimed every 7 days — no staking, nothing to sign up for.
  • Liquidity0.5%
    Goes back into the trading pool automatically. The LP tokens it creates are burned.
  • Burn0.5%
    Permanently removed from supply on an ongoing basis — the supply only ever shrinks from here.
1% tax on sells
  • Charity1%
    That 1% goes to charity. Nobody enters or leaves this coin without a child benefiting — even an exit gives.
  • Transfers0%
    Moving G2G between your own wallets is always free.
  • Total tax8%
    That’s the whole structure — 7% on buys, 1% on sells, 0% on transfers. Nothing hidden, nothing else.
No limits. The old restrictions are gone — no max buy, no max wallet, no trading cooldown, no blacklist, no whitelist. The contract stays plain and open.

How holders are made whole

The airdrop: 100 new for every 1 you hold

Every qualified wallet receives 100 new G2G for every 1 G2G it holds on airdrop day, up to the balance recorded in our holder review. Because the new supply is exactly 100× the old, holding carries your share over in full — same slice of the project, new engine.

Who qualifies: wallets from our holder review that have been active in the last 30 days — activity means moving G2G or any other coin on the Base network. Wallets quiet for over a month are skipped; they keep their old tokens, they just don’t receive the airdrop.

You do nothing. The airdrop comes to your wallet on its own. There is no form, no claim link, and nothing to connect — see the safety section below.

Your old coins stay yours

The original coin keeps trading. Its liquidity — about $6,000 — stays locked, with the largest lock running until March 2029. You can verify every lock on PinkSale. Selling is your right, today and always.

One thing to know plainly: the airdrop follows the holders. A wallet that sells out before the airdrop receives nothing. That is not a punishment — it is simply who this reconciliation is for.

And when the old liquidity unlocks in March 2029, the plan is to move it into the new coin. That is when the transition completes.

Questions

Straight answers

Do I need to do anything to get the airdrop?
No. If your wallet qualifies — it was in our holder review and active on Base in the last 30 days — the new coins arrive in the same wallet automatically. No forms, no claim links, no connecting your wallet anywhere. Anyone asking you to “claim” or “migrate” is a scammer.
What happens to my old G2G?
Nothing changes. The original coin keeps trading, its liquidity stays locked until March 2029, and your coins stay in your wallet. When that liquidity unlocks in 2029, the plan is to move it into the new coin. Just remember: the airdrop is based on what you hold on airdrop day — a wallet that sells out before then receives nothing.
When does the new coin start trading?
When the launch sequence above reaches its final steps — liquidity added and locked, airdrop landed — and not a moment before. There is no countdown forcing it; trading stays disabled until we open it deliberately, and a test buy at open proves the giving engine works. The date and the contract address will be announced only in our official Telegram, on grind2give.org, and on @grind2givedev on X.
Did anyone lose money because of the old contract?
No. Every buyer received 100% of the coins they purchased — the tax was never taken at all. What was lost was the giving: the charity contributions and holder dividends the tax was supposed to produce. That is exactly what Born Again restores.
Why is there a 1% sell tax now?
Because that’s where a lot of the movement is — and every bit of it goes to charity. Under the old design, selling gave the kids nothing. Now nobody enters or leaves this coin without a child benefiting. Entering got cheaper at the same time: the buy tax dropped from 10% to 7%.
How do the new dividends work?
Hold at least 254,000 G2G and you’re eligible. Your share of the 2% dividend tax is paid to you in ETH automatically — auto-claim runs every 7 days. No staking, no claiming, nothing to sign up for.
Where does the charity money go?
To the public charity wallet shown above, in ETH, automatically — 4% from buys and 1% from sells, fired by the contract itself once the accumulated taxes reach 0.1% of the liquidity pool. From there it funds care and research for kids fighting cancer at a hospital where families are never billed for treatment. Every movement is on-chain and verifiable. The hospital is not affiliated with Grind2Give and does not endorse the token — supporting its work is our choice, made because of the impact it already has.
Is this financial advice?
No. Nothing on this page is financial advice. Crypto is extremely volatile — do your own research, never spend what you can’t afford to lose, and never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.
