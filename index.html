Every time $G2G is bought

10% Buy Tax only — Sells and transfers are 0%. The 10% collected on buys is converted and sent directly to a leading pediatric cancer research hospital to support their lifesaving research and treatment of childhood cancer and other life-threatening diseases.

How the tax is sent: Taxes accumulate until a threshold is reached (0.5% of the token balance in the liquidity pool). Once that threshold is met, the collected tax is automatically swapped and sent to the charity wallet. This keeps gas costs low and ensures funds are delivered efficiently.
Important note: This decision to support a leading pediatric cancer research hospital is made solely by the Grind2Give project. The hospital is not affiliated with, does not endorse, and has no involvement in the $G2G token or this cryptocurrency project. We simply chose to direct the tax proceeds to their work because of the impact they are already making.

The recipient hospital treats children with cancer and other life-threatening diseases at no cost to families. Your grind helps fund research, treatment, and care so no family ever receives a bill.
