Phase 1 Rules & Guidelines

These rules apply after you have officially been selected and accepted as a G2G Ambassador.

1. Phase 1 Mission

As an approved G2G Ambassador, your Phase 1 assignment is to create one original post about Grind2Give (G2G) on X and represent the G2G community authentically.

2. Original Content

Your qualifying post must:

  • Be written in your own words
  • Reflect your honest opinion
  • Be directly related to Grind2Give/G2G
  • Remain publicly visible during the required period
  • Not copy another Ambassador's content word-for-word

3. Required Ambassador Disclosure

Because you will receive G2G for completing the Ambassador assignment, your qualifying post must include:

#ad | G2G Ambassador. I receive G2G rewards for participating in the Grind2Give Ambassador Program.

The disclosure must remain visible with the qualifying post for the entire Phase 1 period.

4. Seven-Day Pin Requirement

Your qualifying G2G post must remain pinned to your X profile for 7 consecutive days. If the post is removed or unpinned before the seven-day requirement is completed, Phase 1 is not complete.

5. Public Post Requirement

Your qualifying post and X profile must remain publicly accessible during the seven-day period so Grind2Give can verify completion.

6. What You Can Talk About

  • The Grind2Give mission
  • Why G2G interests you
  • The G2G community
  • Your personal perspective on G2G
  • Community participation
  • Holder rewards
  • Token burns
  • Purpose and growth
  • The meaning behind Grind. Give. Grow.

Use your own voice. We want G2G Ambassadors, not copy-and-paste advertisements.

7. No Guaranteed Financial Claims

Ambassadors must not promise or imply guaranteed financial results. Do not make claims such as “G2G is guaranteed to go up,” “You're guaranteed to make money,” or any guaranteed price prediction or investment-return claim.

8. No Artificial Engagement

Ambassadors must not use bots, fake accounts, purchased followers, purchased likes or reposts, fake comments, spam, or other methods intended to create misleading popularity.

9. No Required Trading

Ambassadors are not required to buy, sell, or trade G2G to complete Phase 1. Ambassadors must not create artificial trading activity for the purpose of making G2G appear more active.

10. One Ambassador Position Per Person

Each individual is permitted one Phase 1 Ambassador position and one Phase 1 completion reward. Using multiple identities, X accounts, or wallets may result in disqualification.

11. Wallet Safety

Only your public Base wallet address is needed to receive your G2G reward. Grind2Give will never ask for seed phrases, private keys, recovery phrases, wallet passwords, or login credentials.

12. Platform Rules

Ambassador content must comply with the rules and policies of X. Participation in the G2G Ambassador Program does not override any social-media platform’s rules.

13. Phase 1 Verification

After completing the full seven-day requirement, provide your qualifying X post for verification.

14. Phase 1 Reward

After successful completion and verification of all Phase 1 requirements, the Ambassador will receive 50,000 $G2G. The reward is paid in G2G cryptocurrency. No specific dollar value, profit, or investment return is promised or guaranteed.

15. Completion Is Required

Being selected as a G2G Ambassador does not automatically earn the reward. The 50,000 G2G reward is earned only after all Phase 1 requirements have been successfully completed and verified.

16. Age Requirement

All G2G Ambassadors must be 18 years of age or older.

17. Future Phases

Successful completion of Phase 1 does not automatically guarantee acceptance into another Ambassador phase, additional rewards, future Ambassador positions, or employment with Grind2Give.
