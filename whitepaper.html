Abstract

Grind2Give ($G2G) is a community-first ERC-20 token on Base built around a single idea: everyday trading activity can be turned into consistent, transparent support for children fighting cancer and other life-threatening diseases. Every buy of $G2G carries a 10% tax that is split between charity (8%), holder dividends (1.5%), and an automatic burn (0.5%).

The charity portion accumulates until it reaches 0.5% of the liquidity pool balance, then the contract swaps the collected tokens and sends the resulting ETH to a public charity wallet. The flow is visible to anyone who looks at the chain.

How the Tax Works

The 10% buy tax is split as follows: 8% to charity, 1.5% to eligible holders as dividends, and 0.5% automatically burned. Sells and transfers carry no tax.

All taxes accumulate until they reach 0.5% of the token balance in the liquidity pool, then are automatically processed.
