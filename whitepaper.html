$G2G on Base

Abstract

Grind2Give ($G2G) is a community token on Base that converts everyday trading volume into transparent support for children facing life-threatening illnesses. A 10% tax on buys is automatically collected, swapped, and sent to a public charity wallet once a threshold is reached. Sells and transfers remain tax-free. Liquidity is locked, anti-whale limits are in place, and all charity flow is verifiable on-chain.

Why $G2G?

Most tokens are built for extraction. $G2G was built for contribution. After watching too many projects take from people and leave nothing behind, we created a token where participation itself generates something useful. Every buy moves real value toward children who need care, while holders retain full control of their tokens. The design is simple on purpose: clear rules, public wallets, and no hidden mechanisms.

Why This Cause?

Childhood cancer and other life-threatening pediatric illnesses create immense financial and emotional pressure on families. Many cannot afford the care their children need. Grind2Give chose this focus because the need is urgent, measurable, and ongoing. The project does not claim affiliation with any hospital. The charity wallet is public, the transfers are automatic, and anyone can verify where the funds go. Supporting children in crisis is the outcome we chose to pursue with the token’s mechanics.

Overview

Grind2Give ($G2G) is a community-driven token on the Base blockchain designed to turn ordinary trading activity into transparent, on-chain support for children facing life-threatening illnesses. Every buy contributes to a public charity wallet. The mechanism is automatic, verifiable, and requires no trust beyond the smart contract.

Tokenomics

Buy Tax

A 10% tax is applied on every buy. The tax is collected in $G2G and held by the contract until it reaches the swap threshold (0.5% of the token balance in the liquidity pool). Once the threshold is met, the collected tokens are automatically swapped for ETH and sent to the public charity wallet.

Sells and Transfers

Sells and wallet-to-wallet transfers carry 0% tax.

Anti-Whale Protections

  • Maximum buy per transaction: 1,200,000 $G2G
  • Maximum wallet holding: 2,540,000 $G2G
  • No cooldown between transactions

Supply & Liquidity

  • Total supply: 25,400,000 $G2G
  • Approximately 80% of supply allocated to liquidity and locked on PinkSale
  • Trading pair: Uniswap V2 on Base

Charity Mechanism

The 10% buy tax accumulates in the contract. When the balance reaches 0.5% of the liquidity pool’s token balance, the contract automatically swaps the collected tokens for ETH and sends the proceeds to the public charity wallet:

0x92EE2370b56DC32794A6CD72585dC01d4288D314 (Ethereum mainnet)

All transfers are visible on-chain. The charity wallet is public and can be monitored by anyone. The project team does not control these funds once they are sent.

This decision to support childhood cancer research and treatment is made solely by the Grind2Give project. No hospital or organization is affiliated with, endorses, or has any involvement in the $G2G token.

Security & Ownership

  • Liquidity locked on PinkSale
  • Contract ownership held by a multi-sig wallet
  • No mint function
  • No blacklist or pause functions

Risks

  • Smart contract risk: The contract has not undergone a formal third-party audit. Bugs or vulnerabilities could exist.
  • Liquidity risk: Trading volume and liquidity depth may be limited, affecting the ability to buy or sell at desired prices.
  • Regulatory uncertainty: Cryptocurrency regulation continues to evolve. Future rules could affect the token or its participants.
