Abstract

Grind2Give ($G2G) is a community-first ERC-20 token on Base built around a single idea: everyday trading activity can be turned into consistent, transparent support for children fighting cancer and other life-threatening diseases. What began as a simple desire to give from the heart has grown into a focused on-chain mechanism. Every buy of $G2G carries a 10% tax that is split between charity, holder dividends, and an automatic burn.

Most cryptocurrencies are designed solely to capture value for holders or traders. Most charity tokens rely on manual processes, periodic decisions, or off-chain promises. Grind2Give takes a different approach. The 10% buy tax is written into the contract and split as follows: 8% to charity, 1.5% to eligible holders as dividends, and 0.5% automatically burned. The charity portion accumulates until it reaches 0.5% of the liquidity pool balance, then the contract swaps the collected tokens and sends the resulting ETH to a public charity wallet. No one has to remember to donate. No vote is required. The flow is visible to anyone who looks at the chain.

Trust in this model rests on two things that can be checked independently. First, the recipient is a long-standing, publicly accountable institution whose mission—treating children with cancer and other life-threatening diseases at no cost to families—is documented and audited by third parties. Second, the Grind2Give contract and charity wallet are open. Anyone can verify that tax is collected, that it accumulates, and that it is sent to the published address. There is no private wallet, no discretionary hold, and no claim that cannot be confirmed on Basescan or PinkSale.

Why This Recipient

A leading pediatric cancer research hospital was chosen because its mission is specific, measurable, and publicly documented. The hospital treats children with cancer and other life-threatening diseases, and it does so at no cost to families. Research, treatment, and care are funded so that no parent ever receives a bill for their child’s care. That model has been in place for decades and is supported by a large, ongoing base of donors and institutional oversight.

Several factors support trust in the recipient:

  • Public accountability. The institution publishes financial reports, is subject to independent audits, and maintains a long public record of how donations are used. Its work is not a private claim; it is an institution whose operations can be examined by anyone.
  • Clear outcome. The goal is concrete: children receive treatment without their families facing medical bills, and research continues into better therapies. That outcome is easier to evaluate than vague “support” or open-ended grant programs.
  • Established track record. The hospital has operated for more than sixty years. Its survival rates for certain childhood cancers and its no-cost model are widely reported and independently reviewed. It has helped raise the overall U.S. childhood cancer survival rate from 20% in 1962 to more than 80% today.
  • No cost to families. Families never receive a bill for treatment, travel, housing, or food. The focus stays on the child’s care rather than financial burden.
  • Research focus. It conducts and shares research that advances treatment for childhood cancer worldwide, not only for the children it treats directly.
  • Transparent finances. Annual reports and Form 990 filings are public. Donors can see how funds are allocated between research, treatment, and operations.
  • No affiliation required. The institution does not need to partner with or endorse a cryptocurrency project for funds to be useful. A donation to its published channels is used under the same rules that govern any other contribution.

This decision to support the recipient is made solely by the Grind2Give project. The hospital is not affiliated with, does not endorse, and has no involvement in the $G2G token. The project simply routes a portion of trading activity to an organization whose work and finances are already public.

How the Tax Works

The 10% buy tax is split as follows: 8% to charity, 1.5% to eligible holders as dividends, and 0.5% automatically burned. Sells and transfers carry no tax. The charity portion is not sent on every individual trade. Instead, it accumulates inside the contract until a threshold is met.

The 0.5% Threshold

The charity portion of the tax is held until the accumulated amount reaches 0.5% of the token balance in the liquidity pool. Once that level is reached, the contract automatically swaps the collected tokens for ETH and sends the proceeds to the charity wallet. This approach reduces gas costs and ensures that transfers are large enough to be meaningful rather than fragmented across many tiny transactions.

Example: If the liquidity pool holds 1,000,000 $G2G, the threshold is 5,000 $G2G in accumulated charity tax. When that amount is reached, the contract swaps and sends the resulting ETH to the charity wallet. Until the threshold is hit, the charity tax continues to accumulate with each buy.

Anyone can verify the charity wallet balance and incoming transactions on Basescan at any time.

Token Utility

$G2G is a utility token whose primary function is to route a portion of trading activity toward charity while also rewarding holders and reducing supply over time. Holding or trading the token does not confer governance rights or equity in any entity. Its utility is defined by the on-chain mechanics described in this paper:

  • Every buy contributes 8% toward the charity accumulation pool, 1.5% as dividends to eligible holders, and 0.5% is burned.
  • Once the charity threshold is met, funds move automatically to the public charity wallet.
  • Anti-whale limits (max buy and max wallet) are enforced at the contract level to reduce large-scale concentration.

The token is designed for participants who want their trading activity to produce a measurable, on-chain charitable outcome while also receiving holder rewards.

Security & Ownership

Ownership of the token contract has been fully renounced. No individual or multi-sig can modify taxes, limits, max buy, max wallet, or any other owner-controlled functions. All contract parameters are now permanent and immutable.

What this means: The 10% buy tax (8% charity, 1.5% dividends, 0.5% burn), max buy of 1,200,000, max wallet of 2,540,000, and all other settings cannot be changed by anyone. The mechanism is locked as written.

Risks

Participants should be aware of the following:

  • Volatility: $G2G is a cryptocurrency. Its price can rise or fall sharply, and holders may lose their entire investment.
  • No guarantee of impact: While the tax mechanism is on-chain, the ultimate use of funds by the recipient hospital is outside the control of this project.
  • Smart contract risk: The contract has not undergone a formal third-party audit. Bugs or vulnerabilities could exist.
  • Liquidity risk: Trading volume and liquidity depth may be limited, affecting the ability to buy or sell at desired prices.
  • Regulatory uncertainty: Cryptocurrency regulation continues to evolve. Future rules could affect the token or its participants.
