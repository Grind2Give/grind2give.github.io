Abstract

Grind2Give ($G2G) V2 is a community-first ERC-20 utility token on Base that turns everyday activity into real charitable impact. We have completely revamped and rebranded the project to reach more people than ever before.

What began as a gaming-powered vision has expanded into a full giving movement — now creating books, videos, music, and much more — while still keeping Strike2Give (our original PvP bowling game) as part of the long-term vision.

With a clear 10% Buy Tax sent directly to Greater Church of Acworth, every purchase automatically funds real-world outreach.
