About the Creator

Art Hoskins • Grind2Give

Life Outside of Crypto

I’m Art Hoskins. Before Grind2Give, my life has been centered around faith, family, music, and community.

I grew up in Long Beach, California, and later moved to Georgia. I’ve been married for over 12 years and have seven children. Family is a big part of who I am.

I coach basketball and have always had a heart for giving back. That same desire to help others is what eventually led me to create Grind2Give — a way to turn everyday activity into real support for kids fighting cancer.

I’m also involved in music through Psalms 98.1 and work with Greater Church Acworth. My faith has been the foundation through different seasons of life.
